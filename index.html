Dr. Scott Heggen

Associate Professor of Computer Science

Chair of Academic Division II

Computer Science Department

Berea College

I teach computer science at Berea College, where my work centers on software engineering education, embedded systems, and computer networking. I lead the Student Software Development Team, a group of work study students hired through Berea's Work Study Program to build real software for the college — a hands-on way to learn software engineering practice, not just theory.

I hold a Ph.D. in Computing and Information Systems from UNC Charlotte, and my research and teaching are grounded in making computing accessible to students from all backgrounds. Outside of work, I enjoy the outdoors of Kentucky with my wife and our four kids.
